# PermitPulse Demo

PermitPulse is a public demo of ORCA Framework turning a broad business prompt into a first-outreach-ready microbusiness package.

Repo: [henryvn27/PermitPulse](https://github.com/henryvn27/PermitPulse)

## What It Demonstrates

The prompt asked an agent to identify and make a business that could feasibly bring in profit and take less than one week to build.

ORCA narrowed that into:

- city: Chicago
- buyer: roofing companies
- offer: weekday roof-replacement permit digest
- pricing: $199 setup plus $349/month

The run produced:

- a landing page
- sample alert
- outreach packet
- ops checklist
- legal pages
- GitHub repo
- Notion project structure
- a Python script that pulls and formats Chicago roofing permit data

## Why It Belongs In ORCA Docs

PermitPulse is not a new ORCA command. It is a proof case for `orca-demo`, `orca-idea`, `orca-plan-idea`, and `orca-goal`: ORCA can take a vague outcome, choose a narrow wedge, build useful artifacts, and stop with the business first-outreach-ready instead of stopping at a brainstorm.

## Caveat

PermitPulse was not deployed or proven profitable. Treat it as capability evidence for ORCA's autonomous product-building loop, not as validation that the business itself works.
